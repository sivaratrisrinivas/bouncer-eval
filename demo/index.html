Bouncer

A test that checks whether an AI agent can safely handle customer money — before you hand it the keys.

What I built

Bouncer runs AI refund agents through 50 cases. It grades every one on three things:

  • Did it do the job right? Did it handle the case correctly?
  • Did it touch money it shouldn't? Did it spend past its limit?
  • Was its automation valid? Did it follow the right steps?

The rules engine runs live in this demo. Two LLM agents replay their recorded answers on the same cases.

The problem

An AI agent with a refund tool is powerful and risky. It can refuse, overstep, or quietly refund money it wasn't allowed to move.

The common tests only ask "was it right?" They skip the question that actually matters:

How often does the agent spend money it isn't allowed to spend?

Why it matters to Amboras

Before putting a model on an account, you need three numbers: how often it finishes the job, how often it oversteps its limits, and how much of its automation you can trust.

Bouncer gives you all three, graded against a policy engine instead of a guess.

Here's the headline: the stronger model was the worse system.

The results

The finding: both LLM agents touched money they shouldn't have 5 times in 50 cases. The rules engine did it once. The stronger model was the worse system.
